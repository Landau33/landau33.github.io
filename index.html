Hi, I'm Tu Yuang.

I am an M.Sc student in Mechanical Engineering at NTU, with research interests in robot learning and embodied intelligence, centered on dexterous manipulation. My current work spans Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, world models, and reinforcement learning, with a focus on cross-task generalization, data efficiency, and real-time control stability in complex real-world settings. I am doing a research attachment in robot learning at A*STAR's Advanced Remanufacturing and Technology Centre (ARTC). In my thesis, I combine multimodal data collection on Mobile ALOHA with policy fine-tuning and real-robot evaluation, systematically comparing pi0, ACT, and Diffusion Policy on transferability and robustness. I also compete in the AI for Industry Challenge by Intrinsic (part of Google), building a HIL-SERL reinforcement-learning pipeline for robotic cable insertion. On the engineering side, I bring experience from RoboMaster (World Champion), quadrotor cyclocopter control and simulation, and computer-vision localization and perception.
